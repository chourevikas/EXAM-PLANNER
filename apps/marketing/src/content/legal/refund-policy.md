This policy covers purchases of paid plans on examplanner.in.

## Cancellation

You can cancel a paid plan at any time from your account, or by emailing [support@examplanner.in](mailto:support@examplanner.in). After you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period and won't renew.

## Refunds

- **7-day refund window:** if you're not satisfied, you can ask for a full refund within **7 days** of your first purchase of a plan.
- **Renewals:** renewal payments can't be refunded once the new billing period has started. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged.
- **Duplicate or failed payments:** if you were charged twice, or money was debited for a payment that failed, we refund the extra amount in full.
- **Not eligible:** requests made after the refund window, and accounts closed for breaking our [Terms of Service](/terms), are not eligible for a refund.

## How to request a refund

Email [support@examplanner.in](mailto:support@examplanner.in) from your registered email address with your order ID and the reason for your request. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**. When the money reaches your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

## Contact

For questions about payments, cancellations or refunds, email [support@examplanner.in](mailto:support@examplanner.in).
