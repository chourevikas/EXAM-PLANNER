# Module 11: Resources

## Overview
A centralized library for study materials related to the user's exam.

## Resource Types
- **PDFs**: Textbooks, official documents, and worksheets.
- **Books**: References and recommended readings.
- **Links**: External web resources and articles.
- **Videos**: Lecture recordings, tutorials, and explanations.
- **Notes**: Curated notes (or user-generated notes) for quick revision.

## Organization
Resources must be easily navigable and are organized hierarchically by:
1. **Exam**
2. **Subject**
3. **Topic**
