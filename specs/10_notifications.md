# Module 10: Notifications

## Overview
Keeps the user informed and on track through timely alerts and reminders.

## Notification Types
- **Daily Reminder**: Prompt to log in and complete the daily study hours.
- **Revision Reminder**: Alert that a spaced repetition cycle is due for a specific topic.
- **Mock Reminder**: Scheduled alert to take a Mock Test.
- **Exam Countdown**: Regular updates on how many days are left until the target exam.
- **Missed Study Reminder**: Gentle nudge when the user misses scheduled tasks or their daily goal.
- **Achievement Notification**: Celebration alert when the user levels up, earns a badge, or reaches a milestone.
