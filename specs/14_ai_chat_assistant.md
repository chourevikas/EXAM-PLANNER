# Module 14: AI Chat Assistant

## Overview
An interactive AI tutor available to students to help them understand concepts on-demand.

## Capabilities
- **Explain this topic**: Provide clear, customized explanations of difficult concepts.
- **Generate questions**: Create on-the-fly practice questions for a specific topic.
- **Create revision notes**: Summarize extensive materials into quick notes.
- **Summarize chapter**: Provide a high-level overview of a chapter's contents.
- **Solve doubt**: Answer specific user questions about PYQs or course material.
- **Generate mnemonics**: Help users memorize facts through creative memory aids.

## Constraints
- The AI layer is advisory and personalization-focused.
- It should strictly assist with exam preparation and not deviate into unrelated topics or unsupported features like real-time voice interviews.
