# Module 13: Admin Panel

## Overview
The backend interface for platform administrators and super admins to manage content, users, and business operations.

## Admin Capabilities
- **User Management**: View, edit, or ban users.
- **Resources Management**: Upload PDFs, add video links, and manage study materials.
- **Test Creation**: Create custom Mock Tests.
- **Question Management**: Approve or edit PYQs/MCQs. Manage taxonomy (Exam -> Subject -> Topic -> Sub-topic).
- **Human Review / Quality Assurance**: Verify questions flagged for review or uncertain answer keys.
- **Revenue & Subscriptions**: Manage discounts, subscription tiers, and financial reporting.
- **Reports**: View platform-wide analytics and usage reports.

## Super Admin Capabilities
- **Manage Organization**: Top-level platform settings.
- **Manage Admin Roles**: Assign permissions to regular admins.
- **Review Courses**: High-level oversight of study materials.
