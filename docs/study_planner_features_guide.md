# AI Study Planner & Study Logger — Simple Features Guide

The **AI Adaptive Study Planner & Study Logger** is a personal smart assistant built into ExamPlanner. It helps students turn mock test results into a structured daily study routine, track actual study hours, and stay motivated with daily streaks and automated reminders.

---

## 🌟 Main Features Explained in Simple Terms

### 1. 🗓️ Schedule Explorer by Day (Single Dynamic Card)
* **What it does:** Displays your study plan for any day of the week on a sleek, single schedule card.
* **Quick Day Selectors:** Click any day pill (`[Mon (9)] [Tue (9)] ... [Fri NOW (9)]`) or use the date selector dropdown to inspect that day's scheduled tasks instantly.
* **Auto-Generated Weekly Plan:** Generates your target 7-day schedule across Polity, History, Economy, Geography, Environment, CSAT & Mocks automatically in the background.

---

### 2. 📂 4 Collapsible Activity Category Dropdowns
Your daily study tasks are neatly organized into **4 expandable category dropdown accordions**:
1. 📌 **Diagnostic Remedial & Custom Tasks**: Actionable study tasks injected directly from your test performance reports and diagnostic scoreboards.
2. 📘 **New Topic Study Dropdown List**: Core new syllabus topics scheduled for the day.
3. 🔁 **Weak Area Revision Dropdown List**: Focused revision targets to strengthen high-weightage concepts.
4. ✍️ **Practice MCQs & PYQs Dropdown List**: Practice tests, speed quizzes, and past year questions.

---

### 3. ✅ 1-Click Task Completion
* **What it does:** Every task row features a direct **`[ Mark Complete ]`** button.
* **Instant XP & Streaks:** Clicking completes the task instantly, turning it into a green **`✓ Completed`** badge while awarding XP points and maintaining your daily study streak.

---

### 4. 🤖 1-Click Diagnostic Report Task Injection
* **What it does:** Automatically converts test report mistakes into actionable study targets.
* **1-Click Sync:** On any test report page, click **"Add to Study Plan"** to send tasks directly into your planner's **Diagnostic Remedial & Custom Tasks** section.

---

### 5. 📧 Daily End-of-Day Pending Tasks Email Reminders
* **What it does:** Sends an automated email summary at the end of the day if you have any uncompleted study tasks.
* **Why it helps:** Keeps you accountable by listing remaining pending tasks with subject names and allocated minutes so you never miss a study target.

---

### 6. ⏱️ Study Session Logger
* **What it does:** Record actual study hours and question performance using the **"Log Study Session"** button.
* **Tracked Metrics:**
  * **Time Spent:** Hours and minutes studied.
  * **MCQs Solved:** Total questions attempted and correct answers.
  * **Confidence Rating:** Rate understanding from ⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐.
  * **Notes:** Record quick revision formulas and key insights.

---
